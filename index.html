Standing False Claims Act (Qui Tam) Mechanics:
  • Statute of limitations: Later of 6 years from violation or 3 years from when government knew or should have known, capped at 10 years. Retaliation claims: 3-year deadline.
  • Filed under seal: Minimum 60 days in camera, frequently extended by court order during DOJ investigation.
  • Attorney required: FCA requires counsel to file; case cannot move to 'filed' status without assigned counsel.
  • Public-disclosure bar: Discussing allegations publicly before filing can bar the relator claim; hold flag enforced.

Two-Tier Air Gap Model

Source protection is a structural constraint, not a feature. System holding source identity must be physically or logically separated from case content.

Tier 1: Source Identity (Locked) AIR GAP
  • • Real names, contact details, and employer information for every source.
  • • Accessible only to the reporter(s) directly handling that person.
  • • Stored in an encrypted local file (VeraCrypt) or encrypted note, never in the shared cloud case tracker.
  • • Referenced everywhere else ONLY by a Source ID code (e.g. SRC-WIN-01).
Tier 2: Case Content (Working Tier) COLLABORATIVE
  • • Cases, evidence, filings, FOIA tracker, deadlines, and communications logs.
  • • Can be shared with editors, attorneys, or collaborating reporters without exposing anyone's identity.
  • • Contains no un-redacted source names or private identifying communications.
Risk Level Description Recommended Intake & Comms Tools
Low Risk Source comfortable being named eventually; low retaliation risk Standard email or phone is workable, but still log every contact
Medium Risk Source wants confidentiality; moderate retaliation risk Signal for ongoing communication; encrypted email (ProtonMail); avoid employer-owned devices or networks
High Risk Source needs anonymity; faces serious retaliation or legal prosecution SecureDrop or GlobaLeaks (Tor-based, no phone number); Tails OS for source's device; air-gapped viewing setup in newsroom

Three Build Paths & Operational Checklists

Scale the architecture to your caseload, budget, and source risk level.

Tier 1: Low-Cost Solo Reporter

• Password-protected, encrypted spreadsheet (VeraCrypt container) for Tables 2-10.

• Separate, more tightly encrypted note for Table 1 (Sources).

• Signal for communications; manual calendar reminders.

Cost: Effectively free
Tier 2: Mid-Tier Small Team

• Airtable / Notion for Tables 2-10 with named permissions and 2FA.

• Encrypted, access-restricted file outside workspace for Table 1.

• DocumentCloud for evidence hosting; Signal + ProtonMail.

Cost: Modest subscription fees
Tier 3: Newsroom-Grade

• OpenAleph / Aleph Pro workspace for global corporate cross-referencing.

• Self-hosted SecureDrop with dedicated air-gapped viewing station.

• Relational Postgres DB implementing full Ten-Table model with RBAC.

Cost: Meaningful infrastructure + legal
Source Intake Checklist
  • Assign Source ID before recording anything else
  • Confirm & log preferred comms channel and risk tier
  • Get explicit agreement on use (background, on record, anonymous) and log verbatim
  • Log first document/claim with date, method, and initial authenticity impression
Complaint Filing Checklist
  • Confirm correct venue for type of wrongdoing
  • Confirm filing deadline for specific venue and statute
  • Determine if attorney is required (mandatory for qui tam)
  • Assemble supporting evidence with chain-of-custody hashes
  • Log confirmation/docket number the moment it is filed
  • Set internal publication hold if seal period applies
FOIA / PRA Checklist
  • Identify correct agency and sub-component
  • State request specifically to bound search without missing records
  • Request journalist/public interest fee waiver explicitly
  • Ask for rolling production on large requests
  • Calculate statutory 20-day deadline the day submitted
  • Calendar appeal deadline immediately upon response
