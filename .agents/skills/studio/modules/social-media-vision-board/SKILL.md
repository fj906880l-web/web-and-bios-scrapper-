# Social Media Vision Board & Quant Content Playbook

This skill defines the authoritative visual standards, post archetypes, high-converting copy hooks, weekly content calendar, and mandatory legal compliance rules for all social media posts (X/Twitter, YouTube, Discord, TikTok/Reels) generated by the Regime Trader AI system.

---

## 1. Visual Design Rules & Aesthetics

- **Dark Mode Standard:** Deep dark backgrounds (`#0f172a` / `#020617` / `#05070a`) matching professional terminal standards.
- **High-Contrast Highlights:**
  - Bright green (`#4ade80` / `#10b981`) for demand/breakout zones and positive ROI callouts.
  - Bright red (`#ef4444` / `#f43f5e`) for stop hunts and breakdown warnings.
  - Neon cyan (`#2dd4bf` / `#06b6d4`) for indicator labels, 3D surfaces, and cyan callouts.
  - Purple/violet (`#a855f7` / `#6366f1`) for HMM regime signatures and quant formulas.
- **Annotation Clarity:** Clean callout arrows pointing directly to key liquidity voids, previous swing lows, or order book imbalance nodes.

## 5. Anti-Slop Animation Studio Pipeline (Jake Van Clief Method)

All video and animated social media content is generated using the **5-Stage Disney Animation Studio Pipeline** (`anti-slop-studio` / `generative-media-studio`), borrowed from the 1930s animation studio process (Jake Van Clief, `https://youtu.be/EhWlGingCl0`):

| Stage | Command | Core Contract Output | Review Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Voice** | `anti-slop-studio voice --dir <DIR>` | `voice/audio.wav` (chunks) | Human audio listen gate (rhythm, tone, no slurs) |
| **2. Words** | `anti-slop-studio words --dir <DIR>` | `words/exposure_sheet.json` | Exposure sheet inspection & script alignment diff |
| **3. Cues** | `anti-slop-studio cues --dir <DIR>` | `cues/leica_reel.json` | Storyboard phrase timing review (Leica reel) |
| **4. Scene** | `anti-slop-studio scene --dir <DIR>` | `scene/contact_sheet.png` | Pencil test contact sheet & HTML5 Sweatbox review |
| **5. Render** | `anti-slop-studio render --dir <DIR>` | `render/final_9x16.mp4` | Final video playback & high-CTR thumbnail approval |

### CLI Commands:

- **End-to-End Autonomous Generation (9:16 Short / 16:9 Landscape):**

  ```bash
  anti-slop-studio produce \
    --dir "content/productions/sol_tuesday_roast" \
    --topic "Sol Open Mic Bad Trade Roast" \
    --persona "SOL" \
    --ticker "TSLA" \
    --metric "-$8,400 P&L" \
    --ratio "9:16"
  ```

- **Backward-Compatible Generative Media Studio Toolchain:**

## 6. Mandatory Legal Disclaimers & Compliance Matrix

Every social media post, image footer, video overlay, and thread reply MUST include strict compliance disclaimers:

1. **Not Financial Advice (NFA):** Content is purely for educational & informational research purposes only.
2. **Past Performance Warning:** *"Past results do not guarantee future profits. Trading stocks, options, and crypto carries substantial risk of loss."*
3. **Timestamp & Execution Shield:** Disclose that screenshots represent historic alerts and are not real-time buy/sell recommendations.
4. **No Registered Advisor Status:** *"We are not registered financial advisors, brokers, or fiduciary asset managers."*

### Compliance Matrix

- **Trade Alert / Profit Recap (High Risk):** `"Not financial advice. Past gains != future results. High risk."` (Footer of graphic + main caption).
- **Quant / TA Chart Breakdown (Medium Risk):** `"For educational research purposes only. Hypothetical model."` (Image watermark / alt text).
- **Macro / Breaking News (Low Risk):** `"News summary. No market positions implied."` (End of thread / tweet reply).
- **Contests / Giveaways (High Risk):** `"No purchase necessary. Void where prohibited by law."` (Pinned comment / terms link).

## 6. High-Retention YouTube & Sub-Niche Authority Playbook

### A. "Asymmetric Distribution" Sub-Niche Scout

Conduct rapid diagnostic sprints to identify high-CPM quant subcategories where monthly search volume outpaces high-quality video answers:

1. **Hidden Markov Model (HMM) Trading in Python**: Walk-forward regime filtering vs. naive moving average crossovers.

3. **L2 Order-Book Depth & Dark Pool Microstructure**: Real-time order flow imbalances (QuantPad MBP-10 + QuiverQuant off-exchange data).

### B. "Prestige-Authority" Channel Architecture

Structure YouTube channel headers, "About", and video metadata under the high-converting 3-part framework:

- **Part 1 — Instant Subculture Recognition:** *"Autonomous Python Quant Trading & Multi-Agent Architecture for Active Traders."*

- **Part 3 — Low-Friction CTA:** *"Subscribe to track every live model update, code breakdown, and walk-forward audit."*

### C. 30-Second "Break the Pattern" Hook SOP

Eliminate early viewer drop-off on YouTube videos using a direct-response intro template:

1. **0:00–0:10 (Sharp Pattern Interrupt):** Open directly on the surprising metric or high-stakes problem (e.g. *"90% of retail quant strategies fail because of backtest overfitting — here is the exact math proving why"*).
2. **0:10–0:20 (Immediate Thumbnail/Preview Promise Confirmation):** Show the live proof on screen immediately (the 3D volatility surface, DSR metric, or live trade execution ledger) to validate why they clicked.
3. **0:20–0:30 (Roadmap & Value Lock):** Give the 3-step solution roadmap before the title transition to lock viewer retention through the mid-roll.
